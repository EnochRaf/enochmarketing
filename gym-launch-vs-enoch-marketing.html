Gym Launch vs. Enoch Marketing: Full Service Breakdown

Updated September 18, 202615 min readBy Collin Charles

Gym Launch teaches gym marketing. Enoch Marketing executes it for you. Here is a full service breakdown to help you decide which model fits your gym in 2026.

If you have been researching gym marketing options, you have probably come across Gym Launch and wondered whether it is worth the investment — or whether a done-for-you agency like Enoch Marketing would serve you better. These two are not really competing for the same job. One teaches you how to market your gym. The other does the marketing for you. Understanding that distinction before you spend a dollar on either will save you a lot of time and frustration.

This breakdown covers what each option actually delivers, what it costs, who it fits, and where each one falls short.

What Gym Launch Actually Is

Gym Launch is a coaching and consulting program, not a marketing agency. Depending on the engagement level, the program charges between $18,000 and $40,000 or more. What you are buying is education, frameworks, and mentorship — not campaign execution.

That is not a criticism. For a gym owner who wants to build internal marketing skills and run their own acquisition system long-term, the coaching model has real value. But you should go in with clear eyes: Gym Launch does not run your ads, build your website, manage your Google Business Profile, or follow up with your leads. That work still falls on you or someone you hire separately.

Alex Hormozi, who built the brand's early reputation, has stepped away from the program. The coaching and curriculum continue, but the name recognition that drove much of its credibility is no longer attached to day-to-day delivery.

There is also no proprietary client portal, no AI-assisted lead follow-up, and no ongoing ad management included in the program.

What Enoch Marketing Actually Is

Enoch Marketing is a done-for-you gym marketing agency. Under one retainer, the agency runs the full member acquisition funnel — local SEO, Google Business Profile management, paid advertising on Meta and Google, AI-assisted email and SMS lead follow-up, done-for-you social content, and conversion websites built for both traditional search and AI platforms like ChatGPT.

The core value proposition is straightforward: one accountable partner instead of a paid-ads freelancer, a separate web developer, and a CRM no one is actually managing. That fragmented vendor situation is the most common pain point among independent gym owners with 80 to 200 members and $10,000 to $25,000 in monthly revenue.

Every client gets access to Enoch Portal, a private dashboard showing the live roadmap, lead pipeline, ad spend, and delivery updates in real time. Poor agency communication is one of the most documented complaints gym owners have about working with marketing vendors — and no other identified competitor in the gym marketing space prominently features a real-time reporting portal like this.

Enoch Scout handles automated prospect follow-up, routing qualified conversations toward an intro call while escalating judgment-sensitive situations to the gym's own team. It is not full automation — human handoff is built into the workflow for conversations that require real judgment. That distinction matters when you are trying to convert a skeptical prospect who has already visited three gyms.

Gym Recon, currently in private beta, is a revenue intelligence tool that surfaces billing risk, member churn, ad performance, and prioritized recovery actions. It is not yet generally available, but it signals where the agency's product development is headed: retention and revenue health, not just top-of-funnel lead volume.

Pricing: What You Are Actually Paying For

Gym Launch charges $18,000 to $40,000 or more for its program. That is a front-loaded investment in education. Whether you recover that cost depends entirely on your ability to execute what you learn, your market, and how quickly you can apply the frameworks.

Enoch Marketing operates on a flat-rate monthly retainer across four tiers:

  • Get Found — $1,500/month
  • Fill the Floor — $2,500/month
  • Growth Engine — $4,500/month
  • Custom Growth — custom scoped proposal

All plans start with a 90-day growth cycle, then continue month-to-month. Advertising spend and third-party software costs are billed separately from plan fees and require your approval before any spend is committed.

The pricing is published — which is not common in this category. Most gym marketing agencies do not disclose rates publicly, making comparison shopping harder than it needs to be. Knowing what you will pay before you get on a call is a basic form of respect for your time.

At the $2,500/month tier, a gym owner is paying for full-funnel execution — ads, SEO, content, follow-up, and reporting — rather than for the knowledge of how to do those things themselves. For an owner who is also the head coach and does not have 20 hours a week to spend on marketing, that distinction is the whole decision.

Who Each Option Fits

Gym Launch fits you if

You want to build internal marketing skills

  • You want to develop internal marketing skills and eventually run your own acquisition system
  • You have time and energy to implement what you learn
  • You have a team member who can execute campaigns under your direction
  • You are comfortable with a large upfront investment before seeing results
Enoch Marketing fits you if

You want marketing done for you

  • You want marketing done for you, not taught to you
  • Your growth has plateaued and you need consistent new-member flow without adding to your personal workload
  • You are managing disconnected vendors with no single party accountable for results
  • Your marketing budget is in the $1,500 to $4,500/month range and you want full-funnel coverage within it
  • You want real-time visibility into what your agency is doing and what it is producing

The honest answer is that some gym owners would benefit from both at different stages. If you are in year one and want to understand how gym marketing works, a coaching program makes sense. If you are in year three with 120 members and stuck, you probably do not need more education — you need execution.

The Speed-to-Lead Problem Neither Model Can Ignore

Regardless of which option you choose, there is one thing worth understanding: how quickly you respond to a new lead is one of the primary reasons leads go cold in gym member acquisition. A prospect who fills out a form on Monday and hears back on Wednesday has already visited a competitor.

This is the operational gap Enoch Scout is built to close. The automated follow-up workflow contacts new leads quickly and routes qualified conversations toward a booking, while flagging anything that needs a human response. For a gym owner who is on the floor coaching from 5 AM to 7 PM, that kind of system is not a luxury — it is the difference between a lead converting and a lead disappearing.

Gym Launch teaches you why speed-to-lead matters. Enoch Marketing builds the system that handles it.

What the Gym Market Looks Like Right Now

Context matters when you are deciding how much to invest in marketing. Pilotium reported in 2026 that over 73 million Americans hold a gym membership, with U.S. gym membership growing 8.2% since 2023. That is a growing market — but it also means more competition for the same local prospects. Independent gyms and CrossFit affiliates are not just competing with each other. They are up against big-box chains, boutique studios, and online fitness programs all targeting the same people.

In that environment, showing up in local search, running ads that match what your landing page says, and following up with leads before a competitor does are not optional activities. They are the table stakes for staying competitive.

A Note on Accountability

One of the most common complaints gym owners have about marketing agencies is communication — or the lack of it. You pay a retainer, you see some activity, and then you spend time chasing someone for a report that tells you what happened last month.

Enoch Portal is a direct response to that problem. The dashboard shows your live lead pipeline, current ad spend, roadmap progress, and next actions without you having to ask. That kind of transparency does not guarantee results, but it does mean you always know what is happening and can have a real conversation about what needs to change.

If you are evaluating any gym marketing agency, ask to see their reporting setup before you sign. How a vendor handles transparency when things are slow tells you more about the relationship than any sales call will.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Gym Launch run ads for you?

No. Gym Launch is a coaching and consulting program. It teaches you how to market your gym, but campaign execution — running ads, managing SEO, building websites, following up with leads — is your responsibility or that of someone you hire separately.

What does Enoch Marketing actually do on a monthly basis?

Enoch Marketing manages local SEO and Google Business Profile, runs paid ads on Meta and Google, handles AI-assisted email and SMS lead follow-up (with human handoff for judgment calls), publishes done-for-you social content, and maintains conversion websites. All of this is covered under one retainer, with ad spend billed separately.

How much does Gym Launch cost compared to Enoch Marketing?

Gym Launch charges between $18,000 and $40,000 or more for its coaching program. Enoch Marketing's retainer tiers start at $1,500/month (Get Found), $2,500/month (Fill the Floor), and $4,500/month (Growth Engine), with a custom tier for larger or multi-location operations. Advertising spend is separate from plan fees.

What is Enoch Portal?

Enoch Portal is a private client dashboard that gives gym owners a live view of their lead pipeline, ad spend, roadmap, and next actions. It updates in real time so you can see what the agency is doing without waiting for a monthly report.

Is Gym Launch still worth it in 2026?

That depends on what you need. If you want to build internal marketing capability and have the time and team to implement what you learn, the coaching model has value. If your growth has stalled and you need execution rather than education, a done-for-you agency is likely the better fit for where you are right now.

What happens after the 90-day growth cycle with Enoch Marketing?

All plans start with a 90-day growth cycle to build the foundation — SEO, ads, content, follow-up systems, and reporting. After that, the engagement continues month-to-month. There is no long-term contract locking you in beyond the initial cycle.

Which option is better for a CrossFit affiliate specifically?

Enoch Marketing is built specifically for independent gyms and CrossFit affiliates, with services and messaging designed around that persona. Gym Launch addresses gym owners more broadly. If you are a CrossFit affiliate owner who wants an agency that speaks your language and executes the full funnel for you, Enoch Marketing is the more targeted option.

The Bottom Line

Gym Launch and Enoch Marketing are solving different problems. Gym Launch gives you the knowledge to market your gym. Enoch Marketing does the marketing for you. If you are an owner-operator who is also the head coach, already stretched thin, and looking for consistent new-member flow without adding to your workload, the execution model is almost certainly the better fit. If you want to build skills and have the capacity to run your own system, the coaching model makes sense.

The decision comes down to one question: do you need to learn how to do this, or do you need it done?
