Straight Answers

THE QUESTIONS GYM OWNERS ASK.

What types of gyms do you work with?
We work with independent gyms, CrossFit affiliates, strength and conditioning facilities, boutique fitness studios, personal training businesses, martial arts and boxing gyms, yoga and Pilates studios, and multi-location fitness brands. The strategy is adapted to your market, offer, audience, and sales process.
How do you decide what to work on first?
We trace the member journey from local discovery through inquiry, follow-up, booked visit, membership decision, and retention. Then we prioritize the constraint most likely to limit the rest of the system, rather than launching every tactic at once.
We already tried a marketing agency. What would be different?
We begin by tracing the entire path from discovery to membership instead of assuming more ads will solve the problem. That makes it easier to see whether the real constraint is the offer, visibility, conversion page, response time, follow-up, sales handoff, or retention.
Do we have to replace our website or CRM?
Not automatically. We evaluate the tools and processes you already have, then recommend a replacement only when it would materially improve conversion, follow-up, visibility, or team execution.
How much time will our team need to contribute?
We need timely access, approvals, accurate business information, and a clear owner for lead follow-up and sales. The exact workload depends on the plan, but responsibilities and response expectations are agreed before launch so your staff knows what Enoch owns and what remains with the gym.
Who owns the advertising accounts, website, creative, data, and leads?
Your gym should retain ownership of its business accounts, first-party lead data, and customer relationships. Any platform access, asset rights, and handoff terms are documented in the agreement before work begins so there is no ambiguity.
Who follows up with the leads?
The exact handoff depends on your plan and team. Enoch can build the immediate email and SMS response, nurture, CRM stages, and booking path. Your staff still needs a clear human sales process for conversations that require judgment, and we make that responsibility visible from the start.
How do you measure whether the marketing is working?
We connect channel measures to the member journey: qualified inquiries, response time, booked visits, show rate, close rate, acquisition cost, and the downstream business outcomes your tracking can support. The purpose is to find where opportunities are being created and where they are being lost.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. You can work with us month-to-month or choose a longer term that suits your plans. We agree on the scope, price, and terms upfront. The timing of results depends on your goals, starting point, and the work involved.
Does Enoch use AI in the service?
Yes, where it improves speed, consistency, analysis, or personalization. Strategy, creative direction, approvals, and important decisions remain human-led. AI supports the work. It does not become an unsupervised substitute for judgment.
