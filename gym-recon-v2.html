BEFORE YOU START

How does Recon make me money?

The dashboard does not make you money. Acting on it does, through three mechanisms: recovering leads you already pay for but lose to website and follow-up leaks, winning trials from competitors by countering the exact offers and ads they run, and capturing the free high-intent members searching Google for a gym near you. Your plan sequences all three by dollar impact, and the weekly brief tells you the moment something changes.

Is this just AI-generated data?

The pipeline uses serious tooling, and then a human strategist verifies every finding before your dashboard goes live. If a claim is not backed by data we can show you, it does not go in. Software watches, a human verifies.

What do you need from me?

About five minutes after checkout: your address, website, membership price, rough member count, and your biggest frustration in your own words. If you run ads, screenshots or view access help us go deeper, but they are optional.

Do I need to hire you afterward?

No. The plan is written so you can execute it yourself, and most quick wins cost nothing but time. If you do want us to run it, your Recon payments are credited to your first month when you upgrade to a retainer within 60 days.

Why 72 hours and not instant?

Machine research is fast. Human verification and a personally recorded walkthrough are not. Both are why your dashboard is worth paying for, and why we onboard 10 gyms per week, no more.

Can I cancel online?

Yes. One message cancels it, no call, no retention rep, and inside your first 7 days the same message gets you a full refund the same day. We keep the exit this easy on purpose: software that has to lock the door is software that is not working.

What if I am in a small town?

Smaller markets are where Recon pays off fastest. Fewer competitors means each fix moves you up the rankings further, Ad Watch covers everyone in one sweep, and the territory math tells you exactly how many members your area can realistically support.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep every brief and every report we ever sent you. Dashboard access ends at the close of your billing period, and we delete your market data on request. Nothing is held hostage.
