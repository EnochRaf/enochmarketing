Straight Answers

Questions Before You Choose.

The strongest social media system depends on real access, permissions, approvals, and a clear boundary between content work and live gym conversations.

What does done-for-you gym social media include?

The exact scope depends on the selected plan and proposal. It can include strategy, content pillars, calendars, short-form video editing, graphics, copy, scheduling, publishing, reporting, and campaign support. Required filming, photography, travel, community management, and revision terms are defined before work begins.

Do you come to the gym to film content?

On-site production depends on location, scope, schedule, travel, and the selected engagement. Some gyms use a guided internal capture process while Enoch handles planning, editing, copy, and publishing. The proposal states who captures each asset and how often.

How much time will our team need to contribute?

The gym needs to provide accurate information, timely access, real media, permissions, and approvals. We design a repeatable capture and review rhythm to reduce disruption, but authentic local content still requires participation from the people and environment that make the gym credible.

Can you guarantee more followers or viral videos?

No. Distribution and audience behavior are not fully controllable, and follower growth does not guarantee business growth. We create, publish, test, and improve content against agreed attention and business signals without promising virality.

Who responds to comments and direct messages?

The handoff is defined in scope. Enoch may support agreed routine moderation or routing, but pricing, injuries, complaints, exceptions, and sales conversations need a clear human owner at the gym. We do not let sensitive conversations sit between teams.

Can you use AI to create all of the content?

AI can support research, organization, drafting, variants, captions, and production tasks where appropriate. The strategy, factual claims, real member experience, permissions, brand judgment, and final approvals remain human-led. Generic automated content is not a substitute for evidence from the gym.

Will social media generate leads for our gym?

It can create and assist demand, but the outcome also depends on the offer, local relevance, profile, linked page, response, booking, sales, and member experience. We give content a measurable role in that path rather than treating every post as a direct-response ad.

How much does gym social media marketing cost?

Enoch's Growth Engine plan includes the deepest done-for-you social scope at $4,500 per month. Custom scopes are available when production, locations, travel, or integrations materially change the work. Your proposal identifies paid media, software, and outside costs before you sign.
