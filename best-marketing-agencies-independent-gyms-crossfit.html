Short answer: there is no single best gym marketing company for every owner. The best fit depends on whether you need an agency to execute, a coach to teach your team, or software to organize work your team already owns.

This guide compares seven providers that an independent gym or CrossFit affiliate is likely to encounter. The goal is to make the service-model differences visible before you book sales calls, not to force unlike options into a numbered ranking.

Agency, Coaching, or Software?

Choose an agency when the owner lacks the time or specialist staff to run campaigns, maintain local search, build conversion pages, and connect reporting. The proposal should state exactly which tasks the agency owns and which approvals stay with your team.

Choose coaching or mentorship when you want to build lasting operating skill inside the business and can protect time every week for implementation. The value comes from applying the curriculum, not merely having access to it.

Choose software when somebody inside the gym already owns marketing and sales but needs a better website, CRM, automations, reporting, or member-management system. Software can reduce manual work, but it does not replace strategy, creative judgment, or a human sales conversation.

What to Ask Before You Sign

A strong proposal makes ownership visible. Use these questions to compare the sales promise with the work that will happen after onboarding.

Due Diligence Checklist
  • Which deliverables are done for us? List strategy, ad buying, creative, landing pages, SEO, content, automations, lead response, sales, and reporting separately.
  • What will our team still own each week? Get a realistic estimate of staff hours, approvals, content capture, follow-up, and sales activity.
  • What will we own if we leave? Confirm control of ad accounts, domains, analytics, creative files, CRM data, phone numbers, and website content.
  • Which results can you substantiate? Ask for current, permissioned examples from a gym with a similar model, market, price point, and starting constraint.
  • How is revenue connected to marketing? Define the path from source and campaign to qualified lead, booked visit, sale, and retained member.
  • What is the total commitment? Separate service fees, software, setup, ad spend, production, usage charges, cancellation terms, and asset-transfer costs.

How to Choose the Right Model

If nobody on your team can consistently own marketing execution, compare agency or managed-service options first. Ask for a written responsibility map so "done for you" has a concrete meaning.

If your priority is becoming a stronger owner and installing business systems, compare mentorship programs. Protect implementation time before you buy, because the return depends on the work your team completes.

If your gym has a capable operator but disconnected tools, compare software platforms. Test the real workflow from lead capture to booked visit before migrating your member and payment data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best gym marketing options in 2026?

There is no universal best option. Enoch Marketing, Fitness Marketing Agency, and 97Display offer different levels of agency or managed execution. Two-Brain Business and Gym Launch center on coaching and education. PushPress Grow and Wodify are software platforms. The right fit depends on whether you need execution, instruction, or tools.

Is Wodify a gym marketing agency?

No. Wodify describes itself as gym management software. Its platform includes lead management, marketing automation, websites, communication, billing, scheduling, and reporting, but the software is not the same service model as an agency that plans and runs marketing for you.

What is the difference between a gym marketing agency, coaching program, and software platform?

An agency executes an agreed scope for you. A coaching or mentorship program teaches systems that you and your team implement. A software platform gives your team tools and automation. Some providers combine elements, so confirm who owns strategy, campaign work, creative, follow-up, and reporting before signing.

Two-Brain Business lists $1,200 per month for 12 months or $300 per week for 52 weeks. PushPress lists Grow at $329 per month as a standalone add-on to PushPress Core. Several other providers require a sales conversation for current pricing.
