Straight answers.

What gym owners need to know before applying for private-beta access.

When is Gym Recon available?+

Gym Recon launches September 2, 2026. We are currently accepting private-beta tester applications. Gym Recon is fully operational, and approved testers receive access to its complete feature set, including the AI recommendation bot and Scenario Room. During onboarding, we connect Gym Recon to the workflows and data sources your gym uses.

What’s in it for me?+

We hold our products to a high standard and want your honest feedback on Gym Recon. Approved testers receive full access 100% free during the private beta in exchange for quality feedback on its accuracy, usability, and results.

How does Gym Recon work with Wodify, Mindbody, or PushPress?+

Gym Recon is designed to integrate seamlessly with your current CRM ecosystem. It becomes the central action layer across your digital systems and can work with Wodify, Mindbody, PushPress, Google Business Profile, or an uploaded CSV.

Who controls member outreach?+

Gym Recon can send automatic messages to your athletes after your team gives it permission. It follows the voice, rules, and audience your team approves, while your staff remains in control.

What happens after I apply?+

We review your gym, current software, and beta availability. If there is a fit, we will email you with the next step. Beta partners review finding accuracy, test real Recon Missions, and share what feels useful, unclear, or missing. Applying is free and starts with basic information about you and your gym.

How does a gym reach its first Recon Mission?+

Gym Recon connects to and reviews the software and data your gym chooses to share, then identifies the highest-impact opportunities for your team. It recommends the best course of action, and your team decides which objectives and Recon Missions to pursue.

What will AI do inside Gym Recon?+

The AI recommendation bot can:

  • Explain flags
  • Prioritize opportunities by urgency and financial value
  • Draft outreach
  • Suggest call scripts and next actions
  • Summarize cross-system patterns
  • Prepare weekly management summaries

Your staff reviews and approves every communication before it is sent.
