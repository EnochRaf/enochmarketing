The Constraint

A FULL CRM CAN STILL HIDE AN
EMPTY CALENDAR.

Raw lead volume can make a funnel look healthier than it is. The commercial outcome depends on the quality of the inquiry and every step after it: response, conversation, scheduling, reminder, attendance, sales, and onboarding.

Growth leak

The form promises no clear next step

Prospects submit because the offer earned attention, then receive a generic confirmation that does not explain who will respond, when they will hear back, or how to book.

Growth leak

Automation and staff both assume the other owns it

Messages are sent, but judgment-heavy questions wait unanswered. The CRM shows activity while no one owns the live conversation or final membership decision.

Growth leak

Every lead is reported as equal

Wrong-area inquiries, unqualified prospects, no responses, booked visits, no-shows, and new members sit inside one total. The team cannot see where revenue is actually leaking.

Straight Answers

Questions Before You Choose.

Lead systems touch marketing, software, consent, staff behavior, and sales. These answers define the boundaries before work begins.

Does Enoch sell lists of gym leads?

No. We build and improve systems that generate and convert first-party inquiries for your gym through the agreed channels, offers, pages, and follow-up. Your gym should retain ownership of its lead data and customer relationships.

Who follows up with the leads?

The exact handoff depends on your plan and team. Enoch can build approved immediate email and SMS response, nurture, CRM stages, and booking support. Your staff remains responsible for live conversations that require judgment and for the final membership decision.

Will AI talk to every lead for us?

We use AI where it can support analysis, drafting, routing, or approved routine steps. Pricing questions, injuries, exceptions, complaints, and other judgment-sensitive conversations stay with your team unless a clearly reviewed workflow says otherwise. AI supports the process. It does not become an unsupervised salesperson.

Do we need to replace our CRM?

Not automatically. We first review whether your current CRM can support the required stages, automation, integrations, permissions, and reporting. We recommend a replacement only when the current setup creates material friction that cannot be reasonably repaired.

Can you guarantee a certain number of leads or members?

No. Volume and outcomes depend on the market, offer, traffic source, budget, page, response time, sales process, capacity, and member experience. We define the scope and measures, then improve the system using real performance data.

How do you stop automation from annoying prospects?

We define the purpose, timing, frequency, consent basis, stage rules, stop conditions, and human escalation path before launch. Prospects need a clear way to opt out, and automation should stop when someone books, declines, is disqualified, or requires a human response.

Can you improve a funnel that already generates leads?

Yes. We can trace the existing source, offer, page, form, response, booking, CRM, and sales outcomes to identify the first weak handoff. The right answer may be a narrow repair rather than replacing every tool.

How much does gym lead generation cost?

Enoch plans start at $1,500 per month. Paid acquisition and the deeper lead follow-up capabilities begin at higher plan levels, and advertising costs are separate. The proposal identifies the exact funnel, creative, automation, software, and reporting scope before you sign.
