Straight Answers

Questions Before You Choose.

Website projects become expensive when scope, ownership, platforms, content, integrations, and post-launch expectations remain vague. We address those decisions before the build.

Do we need a complete gym website redesign?

Not always. We first identify whether the constraint is message, structure, mobile usability, speed, proof, forms, booking, tracking, or the platform itself. A focused repair or landing page may be more useful than replacing the full site.

What platform will you use to build our website?

The recommendation depends on your content needs, integrations, editing workflow, performance requirements, current stack, budget, and ownership preferences. The proposal identifies the platform and responsibilities before development begins.

Will our team be able to update the website?

That depends on the agreed platform and scope, but maintainability is part of the architecture decision. We define which content your team can edit, what requires technical support, and what documentation or training is included.

Do you write the website copy?

Yes, when copywriting is included in scope. We use your real offer, programs, coaches, first-visit process, proof, policies, and market context. Your team reviews factual and judgment-sensitive claims before approval.

Does gym website design include SEO?

The agreed build can include search-led architecture, crawlability, metadata, headings, internal links, structured data, local relevance, performance, and sitemap work. Ongoing local SEO, content, reputation, and authority building are separate continuing activities unless the selected plan includes them.

Can you connect our CRM, class schedule, or booking software?

Often, yes, when the platform provides an appropriate integration, embed, API, or supported link path. We confirm technical access, limitations, ownership, security, and any third-party costs before promising a specific connection.

How long does a gym website take to build?

Timing depends on page count, content readiness, integrations, platform, review speed, migrations, and custom functionality. We set milestones after discovery and do not publish a fixed timeline before the required scope is understood.

Who owns the website after launch?

Ownership of the domain, platform account, code or design assets, content, integrations, licenses, and ongoing support is documented in the agreement. Your gym should retain ownership of its domain, business accounts, first-party data, and customer relationships.
