Questions Before You Book

CLEAR ANSWERS.
NO SURPRISES.

Is the Gym Growth Call really free?

Yes. The 30-minute call has no fee, requires no credit card, and does not obligate you to hire Enoch.

Is this just a sales call?

We will ask about your goals, current marketing, lead flow, follow-up, and sales process. If Enoch appears to be a fit, we will explain the relevant plan and next step. If we are not a fit, we will say so. The call is designed to be a useful, honest qualification conversation, not a scripted pressure pitch.

Will I receive a full audit or written growth plan?

No. This is a focused diagnostic conversation, not a complete marketing audit, written consulting engagement, or custom implementation roadmap. We aim to identify the clearest constraint we can see and the next decision it suggests.

What should I bring to the call?

Bring your primary growth goal, your biggest frustration, and any numbers you know for leads, booked visits, show rate, close rate, membership, retention, and advertising spend. Exact data is helpful but not required.

Can I book if we are not running ads yet?

Yes. More advertising is not always the first answer. We can discuss whether your immediate constraint is visibility, offer clarity, conversion, follow-up, sales, capacity, or retention before recommending paid acquisition.

What happens after the call?

If there is a fit, Enoch will explain the recommended starting plan, scope, pricing, and onboarding step. If there is not a fit, there is no obligation to continue.
