The Intelligence Loop

Signal to decision. Decision to action.

The Portal makes the plan and handoffs visible. Gym Recon reviews supplied business records for opportunities that deserve attention. Scout organizes approved signals and active inquiries into focused conversations. Together, they make the next decision easier to see without removing human control.

03 // Act

Move with context
