Straight Answers

CLEAR ANSWERS BEFORE THE CALL.

These are the questions that usually determine the real budget, the right starting plan, and whether Enoch is a good fit.

What does each plan cost?
Get Found is $1,500 per month, Fill the Floor is $2,500 per month, and Growth Engine is $4,500 per month. Custom Growth is priced after we confirm the locations, deliverables, integrations, production needs, and timeline.
Is this a long-term contract?
No. You can work with us month-to-month or choose a longer term that suits your plans. We agree on the scope, price, and terms upfront. The timing of results depends on your goals, starting point, and the work involved.
Are advertising costs included in the monthly plan?
No. Advertising costs are billed separately. Your plan price covers the agreed strategy, management, creative, conversion, follow-up, content, and reporting scope listed for that level.
How much advertising budget will we need?
The right budget depends on your market, offer, capacity, channel, and acquisition economics. Enoch recommends a starting range only after reviewing those factors. Your media budget remains separate from the management plan and is approved by you.
Are there setup fees or other costs?
The prices shown are recurring monthly plan fees. If your recommended scope requires a one-time setup, outside software, media spend, or another third-party cost, it will be identified in the proposal before you sign. We do not want hidden costs to appear after work begins.
What happens when we get started?
We clarify priorities, establish the agreed tracking and reporting, build the included campaigns and conversion assets, connect the required follow-up, and begin improving the system with real performance data.
Can we move to a different plan later?
Yes. If the gym's constraint or goals change, we can recommend a different level and confirm the revised scope, timing, and price with you before anything changes.
Do we need every service at once?
No. The plans are designed to start with the support your gym needs now. We recommend the smallest plan that properly addresses the current constraint instead of adding work that is not yet useful.
What does our team remain responsible for?
Your team provides accurate information, timely access and approvals, and a clear owner for leads and sales conversations. Enoch can build response and nurture systems, but your staff remains responsible for judgment-heavy conversations, the final membership decision, and the in-gym experience.
Do the plans guarantee a specific number of members or amount of revenue?
No. Results also depend on your market, offer, capacity, reputation, advertising budget, response time, sales process, and member experience. Enoch commits to the agreed scope, transparent measurement, and disciplined improvement, not an invented outcome guarantee.
What kinds of gyms are these plans for?
The plans can support independent gyms, CrossFit affiliates, strength and conditioning facilities, boutique studios, personal training businesses, martial arts and boxing gyms, yoga and Pilates studios, and multi-location fitness brands.
When does a custom scope make more sense than a standard plan?
A custom scope makes sense when multiple locations, app development, a new website, complex integrations, expanded content production, or another special project materially changes the amount and type of work. We define the deliverables, timeline, responsibilities, and price before the engagement starts.
