The Constraint

MORE LEADS WILL NOT FIX A
BROKEN ACQUISITION PATH.

Paid media amplifies the system behind it. If the offer is vague, the page creates doubt, the first response arrives late, or the CRM never records the outcome, raising the budget can simply make the leak more expensive.

Every channel runs the same message

Growth leak

The ad wins but the page loses

Creative earns the click, then a slow or generic page changes the promise, hides the next step, or asks for too much before trust has been earned.

Growth leak

Optimization stops at raw leads

Straight Answers

Questions Before You Choose.

Clear expectations protect the media budget and make it easier to judge whether paid acquisition is the right first move.

Should our gym run Google Ads, Meta Ads, or both?

It depends on your market, offer, budget, existing demand, creative resources, and capacity. Google can capture people already searching. Meta can introduce an offer to relevant local prospects and support retargeting. We assign each channel a job instead of assuming every gym needs the same mix.

How much advertising budget do we need?

The right starting budget depends on local auction costs, audience size, offer, conversion path, and how much capacity you need to fill. Enoch recommends a range after reviewing those factors. Your advertising budget is separate from the Enoch plan and remains under your approval.

Is ad spend included in Enoch's monthly pricing?

No. Advertising costs are billed separately by the advertising platforms. Your Enoch plan covers the agreed strategy, management, creative, conversion, follow-up, and reporting scope. The proposal identifies any other expected software or third-party costs before work begins.

Can you guarantee a cost per lead or number of new members?

No. Costs and outcomes depend on the market, offer, budget, competition, creative, landing page, response time, sales process, capacity, and member experience. We define the measures, report transparently, and improve the system without promising a business result we do not fully control.

Who owns our Google and Meta ad accounts?

Your gym should retain ownership of its business accounts, first-party data, and customer relationships. Enoch works through documented access. Account, creative, data, and handoff terms are stated in the agreement so ownership is clear before launch.

Do you create the landing pages and ad creative?

Yes, when they are included in the selected plan and agreed scope. We define what copy, design, video, photography, approvals, and website access are required before launch. Your real coaches, facility, and member experience usually produce stronger local trust than generic stock assets.

How quickly can campaigns launch and produce results?

Launch timing depends on access, approvals, creative, page work, tracking, and platform review. Initial performance data can arrive soon after launch, but stable decisions require enough qualified volume and downstream outcomes. We do not set a fixed result date before seeing the market and system.

What happens to the leads after they submit a form?

The handoff is defined before launch. Enoch can connect immediate approved email and SMS response, nurture, CRM stages, and booking support. Your staff remains responsible for judgment-sensitive questions, sales conversations, and the final membership decision.
